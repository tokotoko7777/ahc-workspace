# v22 low-worker two-bank sweep contractor

Standalone exact v7 board shape and capture policy; activate only M≤6. Every four-cell body stance in each of seven alternating odd-row sweeps per bank builds its two adjacent shoulder tiles. This partitions the existing 112 shoulders exactly once, with no new wall or cage. Among eight deterministic bank/sweep orders, use one-off worker-mask minimax DP to assign disjoint contiguous subsequences. Tour duration includes actual shortest paths on the finished shoulder-only board, one action per wall, real individual initial approach and final egress. The distinct opposing worker's initial approach is used to tie-break egress destinations, not treated as a future guard guarantee or reserved job. This distinction matters for deployment, and the observed outputs measure real outcomes.

At runtime each existing immediate/predicted paired job takes priority. Unassigned people execute their own outstanding sweep tasks on current legal board while avoiding planned braces, newly booked walls, and finished bodies other than their starting body. They build only under the original wall legality predicates, skip obstructed/unreachable stations, and let the ordinary free-worker builder repair unresolved walls. Egress tries a real physical exterior stance and releases rather than holds it. Any route remaining at planned nominal makespan+8 immediately yields to normal construction. Full-bank scheduler, rescue thresholds, compact/irregular capture logic, and all M≥7 action paths remain untouched.
