# Initial geometric layout risk: frozen small models abstain everywhere

Independent conditional corpus: first 120 newly generated eligible
inputs (original selector map2, M≥8), no outcome filter. On the same
inputs, map2 and map3 each use unchanged incoming Normal-only with
zero late pairs. All 240 official tester, official playback and
independent score checks pass. Map2 full119, map3 full83; all 83
map3 clears are also map2 clears. On those shared clears the
alternate retains +2,358 total mean-worker cells, but switching
blindly incurs 36 full losses. This is not a native-clock common
validation and not a new scoreboard result.

With six fixed initial geometric features (including pet distance
to gate-safe interiors, blocking of braces, shortest-path crossing,
and a two-distinct-worker stance metric), use four out-of-fold
splits and train-only standardization. Two fixed small ridge models
predict actual score gain and full-clear loss risk. No features use
the hidden judge seed or future positions. All 120 feature rows
are valid. At frozen thresholds +2 million predicted gain and 5%
or less estimated harm, **no case qualifies**; neither threshold
alone admits any case, with the smallest estimated harm ~12.06%.
No accepted switches, no further calibration or native trial or
threshold fitting. This does not prove no causal method can ever
distinguish layouts, but these were not actionable initial cues
for this tested safety rule. Existing distributed result unchanged.

The package contains model source, feature helper, generation,
case-paired scores/hashes and preregistration, not supplied selector
weights or compiled derived solvers. See PRESET and MODEL_PRESET.
