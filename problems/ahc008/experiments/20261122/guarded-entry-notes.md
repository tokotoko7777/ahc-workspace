# Guarded observed micro-entry and one-idle-worker pilot

A local exact finite-state model (guarded_entry_model.py) starts a passive pet at the inside neighbor after observed entry from one door. It enumerates the actual number of independent elementary moves for cows, pigs and rabbits; every leftover-step phase is stored separately. Positions outside an entry are absorbing and capture becomes possible after being at least two Manhattan cells from the door with the guard present. No other pets, dogs/cats, or alternate gates. In a 2×3 room with 4-turn deadline, capture probability with a ready guard averaged .778 cow, .736 pig, .747 rabbit. With a two-turn guard delay these became .685/.665/.636. These are conditional on observed entry, not full-game capture rates.

guarded_entry_trace.py independently records and replays the exact official v3 actions of capture4 on the 30 known development seeds documented in capture-first-notes.md. It scans live animal paths and hypothetical 2×3/3×2 corners before the first durable all-clear sample (10-turn sample intervals) or turn 297. For each animal, it counts *at least one* qualifying encounter with a potentially legal next gate and guard stance. This is an optimistic diagnostic on unchanged trajectories, not a universal cap on different wall geometry: footprints can overlap, workers are not a globally unique resource, and hypothetical walls were not applied to observed animal paths. Actual macro supports and complete boundary metrics are recorded as separate stronger filters.

All 435 animals had some theoretical safe pose; 197 had a human already in position (0/30 all-animals cases), 307 within a move (1/30 all-animals cases), and 224 had both an already-complete macro anchor and a guard within one step. No hypothetical shape had its whole micro perimeter already built. The per-species single-step guard coverage was cow 38/67, pig 62/89, rabbit 75/104, dog 79/85, cat 53/90. Interpretation: opportunistic visits are not scarce in this geometry, but complete boundaries and timely staffing are.

## Operational A/B

Apply capture_micro_guard.patch to a scratch copy of capture_first_submission.cpp; compile GNU C++23. The stateful overlay spends at most one otherwise-idle worker action per turn, before turn 128 and never after selecting a viable refuge. It reuses already built macro walls, shapes a 2×3 or 3×2 local pen with one opening, scores nearby observed species (cow/cat preference) and stances covering multiple potential walls, and does not steal scaffold actions. Legal closure needs the finished perimeter, an occupant, and a currently ready worker; connectivity protection is reapplied. Compile with -DCAP_MICRO_DIAG to reproduce the auxiliary activation log.

Official tester + independent replay on the same dev30: 30/30 cases all-clear for both arms, 219/219 people. Score 297,777,775→237,444,443; simultaneous same-clear union 89.33→71.23, pet-only area 658.53→675.13, largest occupied component 641.40→646.60; one connected human component every case. Max tester wall 0.244544 s, all legal. Diagnostics: 35 choices, 206 proposed extra actions, observed entry counts cow 7 / pig 14 / rabbit 14 / dog 13 / cat 17, and **zero actual emitted micro gate closures**, including after the legality/connectivity guard. Development result under the adoption criterion, no holdout run.

## Next structural direction

Make the wall schedule itself provide most of each micro-pen perimeter instead of introducing multiple extra late prep actions: a notched/diagonally stepped macro divider near already active traffic, with only a single missing closeable gate when the macro construction finishes. Derive the room IDs by connected components of the intended non-flat scaffold (closing intended gates conceptually), preserve a known retreat route for every other worker, then select existing ready guard-to-door assignment only after an animal is observed in the recess. This avoids the measured failure mode (real entries but no completed small boundary). Keep the existing 4×4 capture and ranked tactical score leaders untouched until reliable independent improvement.
